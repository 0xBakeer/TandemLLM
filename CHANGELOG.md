# Changelog

Releases of TandemLLM. Speed numbers are the benchmark row `serve-single-i256-o256-v1` (one request at a time, 256 prompt tokens, 256 generated, thinking off, greedy), mean tok/s with the lookup store off unless noted. [docs/measurement.md](docs/measurement.md) explains the row. Every release produces the same text as the one before it, except where an entry says otherwise.

## 0.3.11-test, 2026-10-02

0.3.10-test 468d669 plus P1 of "One engine for all models" (branch tandem-n1): MiniCPM5-2B inside the engine as the narrator that writes the state lines, off by default. The engine's decode path and `ops/` are 0.3.10-test's; serve.env gets two keys (`QWEN38_NARRATOR_MODEL`, set to `minicpm5-2b` by the deploy, and `QWEN38_NARRATOR_DIR`), and nothing loads at the start. With `ui.narrator` off, which is what every stored profile gets, TandemLLM's output is 0.3.10's and raw is byte-identical.

- A small-model slot in the engine (`engine/generative/`): a generative model with its own tokenizer, weights and KV cache, loaded through the residency planner like the decision models (its own memory pool and stream, the planner's check for its role, never during a hold or beside another load, its measured MemAvailable drop journaled as `loaded`, three failures in a minute unload it), and run inside a time budget: the deadline is checked before every token. The first model is MiniCPM5-2B (`openbmb/MiniCPM5-2B`, a `LlamaForCausalLM`, BF16): the forward is our own plain PyTorch Llama (`engine/generative/llama.py`, eager, greedy, 1,024 cache rows, the longest common prefix of the last prompt reused), no transformers model, no SGLang or vLLM. Its greedy ids equal transformers' on the real checkpoint for 12 of 12 narrator prompts (box CPU, BF16, `tools/narrator_reference.py`), and its logits equal transformers' on a random tiny checkpoint in fp32 (tests).
- The narrator (`server/conductor/narrator.py`, profile `ui.narrator`, off by default). With the state lines on and the narrator on, each line is the narrator's: the step's kind, its meaning and its facts in words go to the model, which answers with one line of at most 14 words and 24 tokens inside the `narrator` role's latency budget (150 ms); a request spends at most four budgets. Anything else -- not loaded yet (the first line starts the load), refused, out of time, at the token cap, too many words, markup, an error -- is the template, journaled with the reason (`extra.states[].summary` and `.narrator`). It runs on the request's thread when the line goes out (before the prefill, between two of Qwen's rounds, after the last token), Qwen waiting, never beside Qwen. A narrated line ends in a WORD JOINER (U+2060) before the frame's blank line, and the strip takes such a line of any text, so Qwen never sees one either. The roles document's `narrator.mode` (`on`, `shadow`: journal only, `off`) and budget apply without a restart. Routes: `GET /v1/conductor/narrator/status`, `POST .../load|unload`, `POST .../try`. The Profiles screen has the switch under "What the user sees".
- The role contract knows a `generative` kind: window `between_rounds` (a generative role only, at most 1,000 ms a call), writes `state_lines` (a generative role only; the honesty rule stands: only the reasoner writes client-visible tokens), contract `generate`. A roles document stored before the narrator gets the built-in `narrator` role (journaled as a default). The residency document claims 6 GiB for it (declared, in the order after the decider); the engine's ceiling leaves its bytes out as it leaves the decision models'. The session monitor's tool-check event carries the calls' tool names and file paths to the state lines (not to the journal). Contract: additive (roles, profiles, seeds).
- `tools/narrator_measure.py`: the n = 1 live check (night 2's recorded events through the narrator at its budget and with the budget lifted, latency p50/p90, and Qwen's decode rate with and without the narrator writing lines, gate 3 %). `tools/narrator_reference.py`: the forward against transformers.

What changes on deploy: nothing in what TandemLLM answers. serve.env gains `QWEN38_NARRATOR_MODEL=minicpm5-2b` and `QWEN38_NARRATOR_DIR`; the server then serves the narrator role but loads nothing until a profile with `ui.narrator: on` asks for a line (MiniCPM5-2B is on the box under `~/.qwen38-spark-engine/models/minicpm5-2b`, 5.03 GB). Turning it on is a profile version, no restart. A rollback to 0.3.10-test refuses a roles version saved with the `generative` kind or a profile version saved with `ui.narrator` (0.3.10's schema does not know them): it serves the newest older version and shows the error in `GET /v1/conductor/config`.

## 0.3.10-test, 2026-10-02

0.3.9-test 94f129b plus two features of branch tandem-g1, both off by default: Qwen decides inside the conversation it already holds (the in-context stage), and TandemLLM's own steps shown to the user as lines in the reasoning (`ui.states`). The engine's decode path and `ops/` are 0.3.9-test's (`engine/cache.py` gains `checkpoint_upto`, which only the stage passes), serve.env gets no new key. With both off, which is what every stored router and profile on the box gets, TandemLLM's output is 0.3.9's; raw (`X-Tandem: raw`) is byte-identical. One thing runs on every TandemLLM request from the deploy: the handler takes framed state lines (`\n› <template text>\n\n`) out of the assistant messages a client sends back, which changes nothing for a request that has none.

- Qwen decides with the conversation it already holds (the in-context stage). A follow-up (a user message after an earlier answer) or a prompt of at least `min_tokens` (8,192) is classified by the engine itself: under the engine lock, right before the turn's own prefill, it prefills the turn's prompt up to its last message (the session's notes replayed) plus a hidden question with one letter per class, thinking off, and takes the class from the last row's logits over the letters (one token each, nothing decoded). Laya keeps short first messages. The turn then resumes at the grid line below its insertion point, so it re-reads at most a grid chunk plus its note and tail; its logits and state equal the turn without the stage bit for bit (tests on the random model, including the drafter's rows and the anchors). The stage takes state-store snapshots only at lines up to where the turn parts from it and no session snapshot; nothing of the question streams, counts in `usage` or reaches the turn. Journaled as stage `qwen_context` (label, confidence, probabilities, ms, its prompt tokens, prefilled and reused) and source `qwen_context`. Below its threshold, failing or not run, the session's class. Switched by the router document's `classifier.context_stage` (`mode` off by default, `min_tokens`, `followups`, `accept`, `question`); off, nothing changes and the prompt is never counted. Images in the conversation go through the stage expanded as the turn's prompt expands them (the same rows, rotary coordinates and cache keys), so opencode's sessions with screenshots are classified in context too, and the turn after the stage is bit-identical (test on the random vision model). `cache.prefill` gains `checkpoint_upto`. Contract: the decisions schema allows the stage and the source (additive).
- TandemLLM's steps shown to the user (`ui.states` in a profile, off by default). Each step the conductor takes is one short line in the reasoning the client already renders, at the moment it happens: `deciding: code_edit, high effort`, `retrieving: skill photo-to-3d`, `compaction seen`, `steering: gate progress`, `thinking closed at the 16,384 cap`, `loop detected, steering`, `empty answer, continuing`, `checking the tool call`, `repairing the tool call`, `asking for the tool call again`, `giving up` (`reading memory: N notes` is defined for later). Templates, no model, no latency; a `summary` field per line for a clerk later. The marker is `›` and a space, framed as `"\n› text\n\n"`; in `reasoning_content` a delta, in `tags` inside `<think>` (a step after the block closed is left out of `tags`); a non-streamed answer carries them in its reasoning. Whenever the conductor is configured, the server strips every framed template line from the reasoning and content a client sends back (opencode returns its reasoning as `reasoning_content`), so the prompt and the cached prefix are what they would be without the feature (tests: the same prompt ids on the checkpoint's template, the same engine prompt and usage through the handler). Not in `usage`, the suffix store or the response cache's key; the journal has `extra.states` and `extra.states_stripped`, the transcript a `### states` block. `ui.kinds` turns a kind off. The Profiles screen has the switch. Only for a profile in mode `on` and `n` = 1; off, raw and shadow stream every byte as before.

What changes on deploy: nothing in what TandemLLM answers. The router document on the box has no `classifier.context_stage` and loads with the stage off; no stored profile has `ui`, so every profile's state lines are off. Turning either on is a config version (Routing › Qwen in the conversation, Profiles › What the user sees), no restart; `~/c310-live.py` turns them on for its checks and restores the versions it found. A rollback to 0.3.9-test refuses a router or profile version saved with the new keys (0.3.9's schema does not know them): it serves the newest older version and shows the error in `GET /v1/conductor/config`, as the 0.3.9 rollback note says for roles.

## 0.3.7-test, 2026-10-01

0.3.6-test plus the engine's memory limits (OPS-47), the narrow drafter arm as a switch (SPD-75), branch tandem-cache-limits, and the eval reader fix with a fresh-text live_check (OPS-63), branch tandem-evalfix. Khaled's decisions of 2026-10-01: a 70 GiB ceiling, a 77 GiB declared peak, capped caches, a parked session; the context window stays 262,144.

- One memory policy for the serving caches (`engine/cache.py` `Budget`). Between requests the engine holds at most `MEM_CEILING_GIB` (70), allocated plus the allocator's reserve; during a prefill at most `MEM_PEAK_GIB` (77). Before a prefill and before the response starts, the free blocks go back to the driver and then the caches, cheapest first (state store, body anchors, guest stash, parked session, pinned head), until the prefill's workspace fits; if it still does not, the request gets a 503 with Retry-After instead of running the box into its out-of-memory wedge. Every cache allocation asks first and may take the room of the cheaper ones. After a request the reserve over `RESERVE_SLACK_GIB` (2) goes back to the driver. Measured in a hold: steady state 67.25 GiB with every cache filled, a 261,611-token prompt peaking at 68.35 GiB allocated, nothing shed.
- The caches' caps: resident anchors 2.5 GiB (17), guest stash 1.0 GiB for guests up to 8,192 tokens, pinned head 4.0 GiB (no longer out of `CACHE_GB`), state store 2 GiB with a 1 GiB entry (room is made before the clone, not after), and the parked session, 4 GiB: when a request of another session (`x-session-id`) replaces the resident prompt, its rows past the shared head are kept, and that session's next turn swaps them back instead of re-reading them (measured: 41,984 of 53,209 tokens reused, 11,225 re-read in 16.4 s). A compaction drops its session's store entries and parked rows. Every limit decides only where a prefill starts; resumes from the park are bit-identical to a cold prefill (tests).
- The residency planner subtracts the engine's growth to its declared peak (77 less what it holds now) before a role's claim, not just today's MemAvailable: at 37.9 GiB free with the engine at 70, a 12 GiB reader is refused (18.9 left) where it was admitted.
- Every limit is a key in `ops/serve.env` (`MEM_CEILING_GIB`, `MEM_PEAK_GIB`, `RESERVE_SLACK_GIB`, `RESIDENT_GB`, `RESIDENT_STASH_GB`, `RESIDENT_GUEST_MAX`, `RESIDENT_PIN_GB`, `RESIDENT_PARK_GB`, `CACHE_GB` 8 to 2, `STORE_ENTRY_GB`, `NARROW_ARM`), passed by `ops/start.sh`. The System view's Memory plan panel shows each with what it holds now, draws the ceiling and the peak over the split bar, keeps the shed log and the last session switch's re-read cost, and edits them as the residency document's `engine` section: live keys apply at the next request, `PREFIX_CHUNK` and `NARROW_ARM` say they need a restart, `MAX_LEN` is locked. Metrics `qse_memory_bytes{part}`, `qse_memory_shed_total{level}`, `qse_requests_refused_total{reason="memory"}`. Contracts: additive.
- `--narrow-arm off` (`NARROW_ARM=0`, SPD-75): the block-16 drafter alone, valid only with `QWEN38_LEN_MODE=wide`, about 8 GiB less. Off by default. Khaled's decision (2026-10-01 20:25): keep the narrow arm, `NARROW_ARM=1` is served; the switch stays an option. The same-hour comparison is in SPD-75.
- `tools/mem_gate.py` (the 262k memory gate for a hold), `tools/verify_spec.py --narrow-arm`, `tools/forced_bench.py` `_nn` configs.

From tandem-evalfix (tools only, nothing the engine serves changes):
