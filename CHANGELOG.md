### A memory note the store refuses is never shown (SRV-277; QA SRV-278)

- In an Open WebUI chat (0.4.3-test, W05) the model wrote a `<tandem_memory scope="project">` block. The chat has no working directory, so the store refused it (`no_project`), and the stream filter sent the refused block to the user as text, by design ("a block that is not taken goes out as the text it was").
- Now every closed block is hidden from the client, written or not: one over 2,000 characters, the fifth of an answer, one the schema or the store refused. The reason goes to the journal (`memory_notes`/`write`, the row's `extra.memory.failed`) and one log line, and the block's tokens are a hidden span like a written block's, so the next turn still resumes at the end anchor. Only text that is not a closed block goes out as written (an unclosed block, one still open after 4,000 characters, `<tandem_memoryx`).
- A project-scoped note in a conversation without a working directory (every Open WebUI chat, a script) lands at user scope (`rescoped: project`), since every client is the one user and a note is meant to be read back in the next conversation (SRV-173).

## 0.4.3-test, 2026-10-07

### The spill entries compressed losslessly (SRV-275; QA SRV-276)

- A spill entry is the model's computed state, not text: 104 KiB a token (the target's 16 attention layers' K and V, 64 KiB, and the two drafters' K and V, 40 KiB, all BF16) and 0.77 GB of recurrent-state anchors an entry. The 182,000-row Open WebUI chat of 2026-10-07 was a 20.16 GB entry.
- New blocks are written as byte planes compressed with zstd level 1 (`engine/spill.py`, `--session-spill-codec`, `SESSION_SPILL_CODEC`, on): the planes are cut and joined on the device, the host compresses 8 MiB frames on 8 threads, one block while the one before it is written. On a real 48,853-token entry the rows go 5.20 -> 3.04 GB (1.71x; target 1.45x, drafters 2.39x), the entry 5.97 -> 3.81 GB; compress 4.5 GB/s, decompress 10.8 GB/s; the read of the compressed blocks plus their decompression took 0.93 s against 0.97 s for the raw O_DIRECT read. `tools/spill_codec_check.py` ran every block of two real entries through the codec: bit for bit. The block sums are checked on the device after every read, as before.
- Entries on the disk are read either way; a 0.4.2 entry written again keeps its unchanged blocks and compresses the new ones. The fingerprint skips the spill's files, so the deploy keeps every entry.
- Measured and not taken: plain zstd (1.27x target), zstd level 3, the fp32 recurrent state (1.17x), 2 anchors instead of 4. Lossy storage (FP8 KV, BF16 recurrent state) is reported in docs/caches.md and not implemented.
- To verify live: a long chat evicted and read back: the save line shows `codec=zs1` and about 0.6x bytes, the restore line, cached 0.95 or more, TTFT within 1.5x of 0.4.2's restore.

### The disk budgets: spill 64 GB, head cache 16 GB (SRV-271 option A)

- The session spill's budget 40 -> 64 GB (`--session-spill-gb`, now passed by `ops/start.sh` from `SESSION_SPILL_GB`, and `settings.memory.session_spill.budget_gb` live); the disk tier's 50 -> 16 GB (code and schema default; the box's settings document was set live before this deploy). The 50 GB free-space floor stays. Compressed, a 182k chat is about 12.1 GB: 64 GB holds about five.

### A daily NVMe write budget (SRV-273; QA SRV-274)

- SMART on the box's 1 TB drive: 11.6 TB written, 1 % used. The disk tier and the spill wrote 137 to 282 GB a day (10-04 to 10-07). `engine/disk_cache.py::WriteGuard` counts both per local day (kept across restarts in `writes.json`); past `--disk-write-budget-gb` (200, `DISK_WRITE_BUDGET_GB`, `settings.memory.disk_writes.budget_gb` live) the tier writes nothing and the spill only a drain and evictions of 32,768 tokens or more; past 1.5x only the drain. Skips are logged (`[disk-guard] skip`) and counted; `/health` `cache.disk_writes` shows the day's GB, the level and 14 days.

### Parallel requests (docs/server.md, "Parallel requests")

- `--parallel N` builds N - 1 side slots beside the engine (`server/slots.py`): each a full sequence (KV, recurrent state, verify graphs, drafter stack) over the one copy of the weights, `--side-max-len` tokens long (65,536); slot 0 keeps the 262,144 window and every cache. - Lockstep rounds: two or more decoding slots verify their trees in one pass (`engine/model.py` `forward_tree_multi`, every projection once over all the slots' rows), each tree cut to its share so the pass stays at or under 32 rows (16 a slot at two, 10 at three, 8 at four).
