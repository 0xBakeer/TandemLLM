# Support

Pick the place by what you have.

- A question about installing or running TandemLLM: [Discussions, Q&A](https://github.com/0xBakeer/TandemLLM/discussions/categories/q-a). Say your version (`cat VERSION`), your profile and your GPU.
- An idea you want to talk through first: [Discussions, Ideas](https://github.com/0xBakeer/TandemLLM/discussions/categories/ideas).
- Something you built with TandemLLM: [Show and tell in Discussions](https://github.com/0xBakeer/TandemLLM/discussions/categories/show-and-tell).
- A bug, a slow number, a model you want supported, or a wrong doc: [open an issue](https://github.com/0xBakeer/TandemLLM/issues/new/choose) and pick the form.
- A vulnerability: report it in private, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) says. Never in a public issue.
- A commercial license: [COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md).

Releases and breaking changes are posted under [Discussions, Announcements](https://github.com/0xBakeer/TandemLLM/discussions/categories/announcements) and in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

Before you ask, the [docs](docs/README.md) and the manual answer most setup questions: docs/setup.md for the install, docs/operations.md for the service scripts and the watchdog.
